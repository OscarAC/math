# Chrystal's Algebra - Worked Solutions

Worked solutions to selected exercises from G. Chrystal, _Algebra: An Elementary
Text-Book for the Higher Classes of Secondary Schools and for Colleges_, Part I
(first published 1886).

These are my own solutions, written while working through the book. They are unofficial
and may contain errors.

The book is in the public domain (published 1886; the author died in 1911) and is
freely available:

- Re-typeset PDF (David Moews' library): <https://djm.cc/library/Algebra_Elementary_Text-Book_Part_I_Chrystal_edited.pdf>
- Scan on the Internet Archive: <https://archive.org/details/algebraelementar01chryuoft>
