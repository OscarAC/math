# Hobson's Plane Trigonometry - Worked Solutions

Worked solutions to selected exercises from E. W. Hobson, _A Treatise on Plane
Trigonometry_, seventh edition (Cambridge University Press, 1928; first published 1891).

These are my own solutions, written while working through the book. They are unofficial
and may contain errors.

The book is in the public domain (this edition published 1928; the author died in 1933)
and is freely available:

- Scan on the Internet Archive: <https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.85807>
